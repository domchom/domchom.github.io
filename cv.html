Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant

Dec 2020 - Present

University of Wisconsin-Madison (Advisor: William M. Bement)

Thesis: Exploring the mechanism of positive feedback between Ect2 and Rho in cortical excitability during Xenopus laevis cytokinesis.

  • Investigated Rho-associated kinase (ROCK) as a negative feedback regulator of Ect2 and RhoA in cortical excitability.
  • Collaborated with a biological modeling group and co-published work distinguishing between two mechanistic models of cortical wave propagation.
  • Co-developed a Python-based pipeline for batch analysis of excitable and oscillatory dynamics in multichannel time-lapse datasets.
  • Developed a Python-based, all-in-one image-processing tool for streamlined image conversion and metadata extraction across commonly used microscopes in the Bement Lab.
  • Built custom ImageJ/Fiji batch processing and analysis tools to enhance workflow efficiency and reproducibility in quantitative microscopy experiments.
  • Served as the de facto lab manager, coordinating supply orders through communication with lab members, departmental staff, and external vendors.
  • Mentored undergraduate researchers in molecular biology and microscopy.

Undergraduate Research Assistant

March 2018 - May 2020

University of Michigan (Advisor: Ming Li)

Thesis: The mechanism of the ubiquitination and degradation of lysosomal membrane proteins

  • Conducted a whole genome high-throughput screening to identify proteins involved in the assembly of the vacuole membrane protein complex Fth1-Fet5 using fluorescent microscopy in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
  • Studied the importance of two distinct lysosomal membrane protein degradation pathways using fluorescent microscopy, ubiquitin blot analysis, and Western blot analysis. Studied the effects of different stress conditions on the general composition of lysosome membrane proteins.
  • Conducted a genetic screening of PY motif-containing proteins and analyzed with flow cytometry to identify new adaptors involved in the ubiquitination of lysosomal membrane cargoes.
