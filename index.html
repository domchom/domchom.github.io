About Me

I am a PhD candidate in cellular and molecular biology studying how cells dynamically organize their cortex in space and time. My research focuses on the molecular mechanisms that regulate these behaviors during cytokinesis, the final stage of cell division, when cells must precisely coordinate signaling and mechanics to divide successfully.


I combine quantitative live-cell imaging and targeted molecular perturbations with custom, high-throughput image analysis workflows in Python to extract quantitative measurements from complex cellular dynamics. This integrative approach allows me to connect molecular-level perturbations to changes in cortical organization and division outcomes.
